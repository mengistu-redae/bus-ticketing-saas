## testing the search endpoint  
##########################

test both from the browser console (through node-bff's /api proxy, using your existing session).

1. Search — as demo-customer (expect success)

Log in first at http://localhost:3000/auth/login 

Then in the console:

fetch('/api/trips/search?origin=Addis%20Ababa&destination=Hawassa')
  .then(r => r.json()).then(console.log)

Expect a 200 with an array containing the seeded trip (Addis Ababa → Hawassa, availableSeats: 10 since the earlier booking was cancelled and freed back up).

2. Confirm the role check now actually works — as demo-operator-admin (expect blocked)

In your other (incognito) tab, logged in as demo-operator-admin / changeme123:

fetch('/api/trips/search?origin=Addis%20Ababa&destination=Hawassa')
  .then(r => { console.log('status:', r.status); return r.json(); })
  .then(console.log)
  .catch(console.error)

This is the important one. Before today's @EnableMethodSecurity fix, operator_admin (which has neither CUSTOMER nor AGENT) would have gotten through anyway, since @PreAuthorize was silently inert everywhere in the app. Expect 403 now. If you get 200 instead, the security fix didn't take — tell me and I'll dig back in.
